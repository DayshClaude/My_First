## Getting good photos with a phone

- Shoot near a window, side-on, on an overcast day. No flash, no direct sun.
- Put the piece on something plain — a white sheet, a wooden board, plain card.
- Fill the frame. Take one straight-on and one at an angle.
- Portrait orientation suits the layout best.

## Before uploading

Save at roughly **1200 px wide** and under about **300 KB** each, or the site
gets slow. [squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) does this in the browser, free —
drop the photo in, set quality to about 75, download.

The filename hint printed on each card disappears the moment a real photo
covers it.
