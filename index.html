About

Made slowly, on purpose

I've been crocheting since I was small and beading for nearly as long. What started as something to do with my hands in the evening turned into more pieces than the house can reasonably hold, which is how this page came about.

Everything is made by me, at home, from materials I've picked myself — natural fibres where I can, glass rather than plastic beads, and proper findings so a clasp doesn't give out after a fortnight. A bracelet takes an evening. A blanket takes considerably longer.

Colours are the part I enjoy most. If you like a piece but not its palette, that's an easy thing to change.

Commissions

How a commission works

Three steps, and no money changes hands until the second one.

  1. 01

    Send a note

    Tell me what you're after — a piece from the collection in different colours, or something entirely your own. Photos of what you like are genuinely useful.

  2. 02

    We settle the details

    I'll come back with materials, colours, sizing, a price and a rough timescale. Nothing starts until you're happy with all of it.

  3. 03

    It gets made

    I'll send a photo or two along the way. Small pieces take a week or so; larger crochet work takes longer and I'll always say so up front.
